BerryBot: Automated Strawberry Harvesting System

Problem Statement

Agriculture is facing unprecedented challenges: a booming global population expected to reach 9.7 billion by 2050, coupled with a dramatically shrinking workforce. This labor shortage leads to significant fruit loss every year. In delicate processes like strawberry cultivation, harvesting is a race against time; delays cause the fruit's quality and market value to deteriorate. The goal was to introduce technological innovation into the greenhouse environment, creating a system that could automate the harvesting process efficiently while minimizing plant stress.

Proposed Solution

We engineered BerryBot, an interconnected ecosystem combining a Raspberry Pi-based computer vision system with an ABB anthropomorphic robotic arm and a Siemens S7-1200 Safety PLC. Instead of relying on complex, resource-heavy AI models, the vision algorithm efficiently isolates red pixels to calculate the center coordinates of ripe strawberries. These coordinates are transmitted to the PLC via the OPC UA protocol, which then seamlessly coordinates the robotic arm's movements using PROFINET. Furthermore, the entire automated cell can be safely monitored and controlled remotely through a custom-built, web-based HMI interface.

Key Results

  1. Achieved 1st Place at the 2022 regional Siemens Automation Olympics (Veneto).
  2. Successfully demonstrated seamless Machine-to-Machine (M2M) communication utilizing Industry 4.0 protocols, specifically OPC UA and PROFINET.
  3. Developed a functional, safe, and remotely accessible web interface for the automation system.
