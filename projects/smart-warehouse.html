Smart Warehouse: Automated Material Dispensing System

Problem Statement

Industrial jewellery and metallurgy manufacturing, including processes carried out by Legor Group, requires the accurate selection and weighing of different raw materials. Manual material handling and dosing are time-consuming and susceptible to human error, potentially leading to inaccuracies in alloy formulations and production delays. The challenge was to design an automated storage and dispensing system capable of accelerating material retrieval while ensuring controlled, precise dosing directly into a weighing vessel.

Proposed Solution

Working as part of an engineering team using the Agile Scrum framework, I contributed to the design and simulation of a custom automated carousel storage system. The design features a continuous-loop conveyor chain carrying modular PVC material containers. Upon receiving a command from the central computer, the carousel rotates to position the required container above the dispensing hopper. Each container was designed with an integrated 3D-printed Archimedes screw (auger), driven by an electric motor to enable controlled material dispensing directly into the weighing vessel without moving the central scale.

Key Results

  1. Developed and simulated a complete 3D mechanical concept commissioned by Legor Group S.p.A. to automate raw material selection and dosing.
  2. Designed a modular dispensing unit featuring an internal screw mechanism intended to improve dosing control, reduce manual handling, and avoid disturbing the weighing scale.
  3. Delivered the conceptual design, mechanical specifications, and 3D animation within a structured, multi-week Agile Scrum workflow.
