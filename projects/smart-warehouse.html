Smart Warehouse: Automated Material Dispensing System

Problem Statement

Industrial jewelry and metallurgy manufacturing, such as processes carried out by Legor Group, requires the accurate selection and weighing of various raw materials. Manual material handling and dosing are time-consuming and inherently prone to human error, leading to inaccuracies in alloy formulations and production delays. The challenge was to conceptualize an automated storage and dispensing solution that speeds up material retrieval while guaranteeing controlled, highly precise dosing directly onto a weighing station.

Proposed Solution

Working as part of an engineering team using the Agile SCRUM framework, I contributed to the design and simulation of a custom automated carousel warehouse. The system features a continuous loop conveyor chain supporting modular PVC material containers. Upon receiving a command from the central computer, the carousel rotates to position the required container above the hopper. Each container was custom-engineered with an integrated 3D-printed Archimedes screw (auger) driven by an electric motor, enabling precise, controlled material dispensing directly into the weighing vessel without moving the central scale.

Key Results

  1. Developed and simulated a complete 3D mechanical concept commissioned by Legor Group SPA to fully automate raw material selection and dosing.
  2. Engineered an innovative container unit equipped with an internal screw-dispensing mechanism that eliminates manual handling errors and preserves scale calibration.
  3. Successfully delivered the conceptual design, mechanical specifications, and 3D animation workflow within a structured multi-week Agile SCRUM sprint cycle.
